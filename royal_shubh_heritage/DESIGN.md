## Brand & Style

This design system expresses modern Indian luxury tailored for high-net-worth individuals, entrepreneurs, and connoisseurs acquiring bespoke VIP mobile numbers, mirror sequences, and auspicious numerology identifiers. 

The aesthetic synthesizes the artisanal refinement of Indian haute joaillerie (delicate filigree, champagne foil accents, tactile ivory parchment) with the razor-sharp precision of premier digital private banking. Every touchpoint conveys exclusivity, trust, cultural veneration, and seamless technological authority.

### Core Principles
- **Luminous Radiance (Light Theme Exclusivity):** Avoid pure stark white or any dark mode paradigms. Rely exclusively on warm ivory, washed bone, and raw pearl grounds that replicate handmade archival paper and polished Makrana marble.
- **Auspicious Geometry:** Integrate architectural micro-jaali lattice motifs as faint perimeter boundaries or ambient watermarks. Highlight numerological attributes (Bhagyank totals, mirror palindromes, angel sequences) with celebratory jewel tones.
- **Institutional Weight:** Reinforce immense value through structural poise, Department of Telecommunications (DoT) / TRAI regulatory compliance watermarks, and cryptographic-grade verified exchange badges.
- **Sovereign Contrast:** Pair deep charcoal-espresso typography with royal saffron and imperial maroon accents to maintain exceptional readability and uncompromised dignity.

## Colors

The palette is strictly calibrated for a warm, high-illumination environment. Deep jewel tones represent sovereignty, fortune, and statutory authenticity, anchored by gilded champagne accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#7A0C2E` - Imperial Maroon):** The sovereign anchor used for high-tier CTA buttons, premium active states, royal tier emblems, and master typography headings.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` - Champagne Gold):** Applied to delicate foil hairline borders, VIP badges, certified rarity emblems, and auspicious sequence backdrops.
- **Tertiary (`#0D5C3A` - Emerald Shubh):** Signifies legal compliance, instant MNP clearance, auspicious numerological harmony, positive price indices, and verification seals.
- **Brand Saffron Accent (`#E65100` / `#F57C00`):** Reserved for live telecast bidding, auction timers, festive drops, and sacred highlight callouts.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#1F1916` - Deep Charcoal Espresso):** Primary text color, engineered for high contrast over warm light backgrounds without the stark artificiality of `#000000`.
- **Secondary Neutral (`#6B615B` - Warm Taupe):** Metadata, TRAI porting guidelines, secondary attributes, and inactive controls.
- **Surfaces & Grounds:**
  - Base Canvas: Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F5`)
  - Elevated Container / Card: Pure Luminous Pearl (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Recessed / Interactive Canvas: Off-White Bone (`#F5F2EB`)
  - Gilded Border Hairline: Delicate Champagne Rim (`#EADBCC`)

## Typography

The typography system operates on an editorial tri-font hierarchy:
1. **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes royal crests, bespoke salon catalogues, and high-fashion luxury. Utilized exclusively for institutional section titles, value propositions, and prestige category banners.
2. **Body & Microcopy (`Outfit`):** A modern, geometric grotesque with wide proportions and open apertures, delivering warmth, clarity, and friction-free legibility across KYC, porting guides, and contract specs.
3. **Numerals & Metrics (`Space Grotesk`):** VIP digits demand tabular discipline, rhythmic grouping (e.g., `+91 99999 • 00000`), and striking geometric symmetry. Space Grotesk renders numbers with architectural gravitas, ensuring palindromes, zeros, and repeated triples align precisely.

### Numerology & Currency Standards
- All currency values must use the Indian Rupee symbol with South Asian numbering formatting (`₹ 12,50,000` or `₹ 1.25 Cr`), never standard Western million/billion intervals.
- VIP number cards must render sequence pairs with fixed horizontal tracking (`0.06em`) to ensure instant visual scanning of repeated clusters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the structural balance of classical Indian court architecture: expansive courtyards, meticulous symmetry, and framed focal points.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column layout, max-width `1360px`, outer margin `3rem`, column gutter `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, outer margin `2rem`, column gutter `1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout, outer margin `1.25rem`, column gutter `1rem`.

### Spatial Rhythm
- VIP numbers are presented inside structured showcase matrices or curio display grids with generous element margins (`space-xl` between logical clusters).
- Micro-spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) separates badges, numerology totals (`Total 9 | Shubh`), and legal verification marks.
- Component-interior padding remains generous (`space-lg` to `space-xl` inside cards) to avoid visual crowding and protect the perception of unhurried luxury.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is expressed through soft, sunlit dispersion, warm umber under-shadows, and ultra-thin champagne metal trims. Harsh drop shadows and heavy dark cast layers are strictly prohibited.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`Level 0`):** Solid Warm Ivory (`#FAF8F5`) layered with faint micro-jaali geometric vectors (`#1F1916` at 2% opacity).
- **Surface Elevation (`Level 1` - Number Cards, Search Bars):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), bounded by a 1px solid champagne boundary (`#EADBCC`). Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(122, 12, 46, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(31, 25, 22, 0.03)`.
- **Raised Elevation (`Level 2` - Hovered Cards, VIP Portfolios):** White ground with 1px gold metallic border (`#D4AF37` at 60% opacity). Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(122, 12, 46, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
- **Floating Overlay (`Level 3` - Escrow Sheets, Filter Drawers, KYC Modals):** White ground with soft champagne border (`#EADBCC`). Shadow: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(31, 25, 22, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(122, 12, 46, 0.06)`. Backing scrim: `#FAF8F5` at 85% opacity with an `8px` gaussian blur.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric balance through refined, architectural curves that evoke arched portals (*Jharokhas*) smoothed into modern pill and card profiles.

### Corner Radii
- **Standard Cards & Surfaces:** `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) to `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`) creates smooth containment for high-density digits and cert badges.
- **Buttons & Search Inputs:** `0.75rem` for compact tools; fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) reserved exclusively for tier chips, category filters, and numerology status badges.
- **Accents:** 1px hairline filigree borders frame cards cleanly without heavy visual weight.

## Components

### 1. VIP Number Showcase Card
- **Structure:** Multi-layered pure white surface with a 1px `#EADBCC` border.
- **Top Row:** Auspicious badge pill (`#FAF8F5` background, `#7A0C2E` label e.g., "Sum Total: 9 (Mars)"), paired with instant MNP clearance badge in emerald green (`#0D5C3A`).
- **Centerpiece:** VIP number in `numeral-card` Space Grotesk styling. Split sequences with mid-dot dividers (`99999 • 88888`). Key repeating digits tinted in imperial maroon (`#7A0C2E`) or gold foil (`#D4AF37`).
- **Footer:** Valuation displayed in Indian numbering format (`₹ 4,50,000`), accompanied by primary action button "Acquire via Escrow".

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign Action:** Solid Imperial Maroon (`#7A0C2E`) fill, pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), subtle inner bevel highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). Hover state transitions to rich garnet (`#5C0620`).
- **Secondary Heritage Action:** Ivory surface (`#FAF8F5`), 1px champagne gold border (`#D4AF37`), Deep Charcoal Espresso text (`#1F1916`). Hover produces subtle gold glow.
- **Tertiary Link:** Clean espresso text with an underline accent colored in royal saffron (`#E65100`).

### 3. Chips & Numerology Pills
- **Filter Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `0.5rem 1.25rem`. Inactive state sits on off-white bone (`#F5F2EB`) with taupe text. Active state triggers Imperial Maroon fill with crisp white text.

- **Auspicious Numerology Badges:** Micro-pills framed in 1px champagne gold border with gold tint fill (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`), carrying uppercase Space Grotesk labels ("ROYAL 888", "ANGEL 777", "GOLDEN PALINDROME").

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- **VIP Sequence Search:** Oversized container (`60px` height) with white ground, 1.5px `#EADBCC` border, focusing to `#7A0C2E` with a faint champagne shadow ring. Numeric inputs support tabular figures and digit masking.
- **Numerology Calculator Input:** Date-of-birth and life path selectors featuring custom warm dropdown panels and gold-accented calendar pickers.

### 5. Compliance & Trust Badges
- **TRAI & DoT Verification Seal:** Structured horizontal pill featuring an emerald checkmark, subtle emerald border (`#0D5C3A` at 25% opacity), and charcoal-espresso text verifying genuine carrier transferability.
- **Escrow Guarantee Seal:** Gilded lock icon paired with champagne foil typography detailing guaranteed transfer timeframes (e.g., "Cleared for Instant MNP in 15 Minutes").

### 6. Numerological Rarity Meter
- Horizontal segmented progress bar rendered in 9 segments. Filled units carry a linear gradient from Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`) to Imperial Maroon (`#7A0C2E`), indicating rarity and numerological vibration.

Frequently Asked Questions

Have Questions? We've Got Answers.

Find quick answers to the most common questions about our VIP numbers, booking process, delivery and more.

A VIP mobile number is a special or fancy mobile number that has a distinctive pattern — such as repeated digits, sequential numbers, mirror numbers, or other easy-to-remember combinations. These numbers are considered premium because they are unique, easy to recall, and can enhance your personal or business identity.

The numbers listed are associated with specific networks as shown in their category. If you wish to use the number on a different network, you can port it using the Mobile Number Portability (MNP) process after activation. Our team can guide you through the porting process if needed.

Booking is simple. Browse the collection, find a number you like, and click the "Buy Now" button. You'll be taken to our checkout page where you fill in your details and complete the payment. Once payment is confirmed, we process the number allocation and keep you updated via WhatsApp.

The price displayed on each number card is the final price — there are no hidden charges. Standard SIM delivery charges may apply if physical SIM delivery is chosen, and MNP porting fees (if applicable) are governed by TRAI regulations. We will inform you of any applicable charges before you proceed.

Yes. All numbers listed on VIP Number Club are genuine, verified, and legally transferable. Each number goes through an availability check before being listed. Ownership transfer is handled through the official operator process with proper KYC documentation, ensuring a completely safe and legal transaction.

We recommend carefully reviewing the number, its pattern, and pricing before completing the purchase, as VIP numbers are reserved specifically for you upon booking. For concerns after booking, please reach out to our support team on WhatsApp — we handle each case individually and will work to find the best resolution for you.

Delivery timelines depend on the number type and process. For eSIM and MNP (porting), the process typically takes 3–7 working days subject to operator processing. For physical SIM delivery, standard courier timelines apply (typically 3–5 working days within India). We'll keep you informed at each step via WhatsApp.

You can reach our support team directly on WhatsApp for the fastest response. Click the WhatsApp button on the product card or use the "Enquire" option — our team will respond and guide you through the entire process, from choosing a number to completing the transfer.
